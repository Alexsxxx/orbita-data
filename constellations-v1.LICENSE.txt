It is an adaptation of the figures of Stellarium's "western" sky culture,
by the Stellarium team, text and data CC BY-SA
(https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures, commit 92df9e4c,
western/index.json). Changes: the figures' lines are kept as pairs of
Hipparcos numbers, and each star is given its position, distance and motion
from the Hipparcos new reduction (van Leeuwen 2007), ESA's Gaia DR3 ("This
work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission
Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium
(DPAC)"), the Pulkovo compilation of radial velocities (Gontcharov 2006,
CDS III/252) and the Gaia RV standards (Soubiran et al. 2018). Built by
tool/build_constellations.py in https://github.com/Alexsxxx/Orbita.

This is the same file the app ships inside it. It is published here, free
of any technical restriction, so that anyone who receives it in the app can
also have it on the terms of its licence.
